## Handoff Rules

The `aidev-plan` skill reads the session file expecting the following sections as **binding context**. Ensure they are populated at step 9 (Craft Handoff) by crystallizing findings from the research phases.

- The `aidev-plan` skill treats `### Scope`, `### Recommendations`, and `### Risks` (inside `## Research Conclusions`) as **binding context** — do not leave them vague
- Every recommendation must be traceable to a source in `## Sources`
- Do not propose implementation approach — research defines **what** and **why**, plan defines **how**
- If the research is inconclusive, explicitly state this in `### Recommendations` and list what needs further investigation in `### Open Questions`
