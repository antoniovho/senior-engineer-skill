# Spike Template

```markdown
---
name: "🔍 Spike SPEC"
about: "Detailed specification for a Spike (research task)."
title: "<insert_title_here>"
assignees: ""
labels: kind/spike
type: "Spike"
---

## 📝 Summary
What knowledge gap is this spike trying to fill? (1-2 sentences).

## 🎯 Context / Background
Explain why research is required and what it unblocks.
- Define every domain-specific term used anywhere in this spec
- Which product capabilities/systems are being investigated

## 🎯 Objectives
List the specific questions that must be answered.
- Each research question must be specific enough to have a definitive answer — not open-ended explorations
- Include success criteria for each objective (what constitutes an adequate answer)
- For comparison spikes, state decision criteria: what measured outcome favors each option

## 🧪 Research Tasks
Investigation activities — what you'll do to answer the objectives.
- [ ] Task 1
- [ ] Task 2
- [ ] Task 3

## 📈 Expected Outcomes
What the spike delivers (recommendation, prototype, decision log) and what decision it unblocks.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
Each criterion must be verifiable — how will we know the spike succeeded?
- [ ] All objectives addressed
- [ ] Documentation of findings
- [ ] Clear next steps

## 🔗 Dependencies
What tools, data, systems or people are required.

## 🚫 Out of Scope
What will explicitly **not** be researched.

## 📝 Additional Details
Notes, diagrams, links, etc.

## 🔗 Related Issues / PRs
Parent Story/Epic or related research work.
```
